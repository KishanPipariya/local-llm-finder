# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Mac Local LLM Finder to be welcoming, safe, and
constructive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable, good-faith feedback and accept corrections gracefully.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or
  sensitive configuration URLs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting form. Do not open a public issue containing
personal or sensitive details.

The project owner may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors
when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
kept as confidential as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
